Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about payments, access rights, and national exam materials.

Can I study for free?

expand_more
Yes, completely! Ethio Exam Hall offers permanent free access to all official past examination PDF papers, national question banks, and answer keys. Premium is completely optional for students who want automated timed mock simulations, step-by-step verified worked explanations, and custom analytics.

How does the Telebirr payment work?

expand_more
When you select Telebirr at checkout, a direct payment prompt is generated. On mobile, it opens your Telebirr app directly or gives you a 1-click USSD push. On desktop, simply scan the generated QR code with your Telebirr app. Your subscription is activated instantaneously without needing to upload transaction IDs.

Are past exam papers official?

expand_more
Yes. All past papers are carefully compiled from historical Ministry of Education (MoE), Educational Assessment and Examinations Agency (NEAEA/EAES), and university departmental archives. Our team of credentialed educators cross-verifies all answers against the official Ethiopian curriculum.

Can I cancel or pause my subscription?

expand_more
You can cancel or pause renewal at any time directly in your account settings. There are no cancellation penalties or hidden charges. Once canceled, your access remains valid until the current billing cycle expires.

What is included in the Aviation Track?

expand_more
The Aviation Track is specialized for candidates preparing for Ethiopian Airlines Aviation Academy tests. It includes spatial reasoning, mechanical comprehension, physics formulas for flight dynamics, aviation-specific English proficiency modules, and structured archives from recent cadet entry evaluations.
