## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, authoritative, and aspirational digital testing and academic resource environment tailored to Ethiopian secondary and tertiary students, educators, and certification candidates. 

### Visual Tone & Aesthetic
The aesthetic balances institutional rigor with contemporary EdTech clarity. It combines a structured, reliable layout with clear modern feedback states. Crisp contrast, deliberate spatial cadence, and refined academic status signifiers replace generic gamified study interfaces, fostering focus, self-assurance, and academic discipline.

### Design Movement
**Modern Institutional EdTech**: Drawing inspiration from structured editorial publications and precision engineering interfaces, the system relies on high-density information architecture, calm visual hierarchies, crisp border geometry, and functional micro-accents. Subtle national pride is communicated through rigorous execution, clarity, and dependable accessibility rather than overt cultural clichés.

## Colors

The color palette is architected to communicate institutional authority, high legibility under timed conditions, and crisp instructional feedback.

### Key Roles
- **Primary Navy (`#10233F`)**: Represents foundational authority and institutional trust. Used for primary interactive containers, core navigation headers, primary CTA button fills, and dominant data groupings.
- **Deep Navy (`#0B172A`)**: The deep base used for persistent chrome, structural sidebars, and critical high-contrast header sections.
- **Primary Blue (`#2457D6`)**: The active functional driver. Applied to link states, active tabs, focus indicators, progress bar indicators, and interactive highlights.
- **Teal Accent (`#16A6A1`)**: The achievement and insight accent. Used for analytical metrics, readiness trackers, percentile bars, and affirmative status indicators.
- **Surface & Background**:
  - **Main Canvas (`#F6F8FC`)**: A cool, low-strain off-white canvas that minimizes eye fatigue during extended test-taking sessions.
  - **Surface Elevated (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white base for cards, examination sheets, input controls, and modal dialogues.
- **Typography & Structural Lines**:
  - **Primary Text (`#182338`)**: High-contrast, near-black slate optimized for long-form question stems and rigorous reading comprehension.
  - **Secondary Text (`#64748B`)**: Muted slate for metadata, supporting timestamps, exam codes, and field descriptors.
  - **Border (`#E2E8F0`)**: Architectural division line for card perimeters, input edges, and tabular grids.
- **Functional States**:
  - **Success (`#15803D`)**: Answer correct, payment verified, document passed.
  - **Warning (`#D97706`)**: Timer expiring, pending verification, incomplete syllabus section.
  - **Error (`#DC2626`)**: Critical exam alert, incorrect response, form submission blockage.

## Typography

Typography delivers legibility across varying display conditions and network contexts. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Manrope`)**: Geometric balance, crisp terminals, and authoritative presence suited for academic titles, section anchors, and test category headings.
- **Body & Labels (`Inter`)**: Neutral, highly legible grotesque font designed for screen reading, long test passages, multiple-choice options, and dense tabular scorecards.
- **Tabular Figures Application**: For countdown timers, test scores, question pagination (`Question 42 of 100`), and analytical confidence indices, apply the OpenType feature `font-feature-settings: 'tnum' on, 'zero' on`. This prevents visual jitter when test timers tick down and aligns numerical columns cleanly across ranking tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column responsive grid engineered for multi-tiered academic dashboards and focused, distraction-free test environments.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Geometry
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column grid, 1200px max content constraint with centered presentation, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 48px (`3rem`) page margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column layout, 24px (`1.5rem`) page margins, flexible sidebar collapse into top utility sheets.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4-column layout, 16px (`1rem`) gutters and page margins. Controls scale to 48px minimum touch heights.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Apply `space-xs` and `space-sm` for internal component anatomy (icon-to-label offsets, segmented filter tabs).
- Apply `space-md` for standard card padding and form row distributions.
- Apply `space-lg` to separate distinct question options or dashboard section headers from content cards.
- Apply `space-xl` and `space-2xl` for macroscopic page architecture (e.g., test section delimiters, pricing tiers, trust metrics rows).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, floating physical layers, and excessive skeuomorphism. Instead, depth is established through **architectural surfaces, border delimiters, and restrained ambient diffusion**.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas - `#F6F8FC`)**: System foundation. All layout groupings reside directly on this canvas.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface - `#FFFFFF`)**: Standard elevated plane for exam tiles, form bodies, document previews, and metric tiles. Framed by a crisp 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) paired with a micro-ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(16, 35, 63, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(16, 35, 63, 0.02)`.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Flyouts)**: Dropdowns, filter menus, exam subject quick-switchers, and breadcrumb overflow menus. Uses a subtle elevation shadow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(16, 35, 63, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(16, 35, 63, 0.04)` over a clean 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
4. **Level 3 (Modal Test Enclosures & Critical Prompts)**: Active modal chrome, time-out warnings, and complete solution panels. Elevated via `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 23, 42, 0.04)` with a backdrop blur veil using `rgba(11, 23, 42, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

The interface utilizes a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. This provides an organized, institutional look while remaining modern and approachable:

- **Base Radius (4px / 0.25rem)**: Used for granular UI elements such as inputs, check controls, radio boxes, small chips, and code/key indicators.
- **Medium Radius (8px / 0.5rem)**: Used for action buttons, filter bars, alert callouts, and document cards.
- **Large Radius (12px / 0.75rem)**: Standard container boundary for high-level exam cards, document previewers, and pricing tables.
- **Capsule / Pill Radius (9999px)**: Reserved exclusively for verification tags, status pills, and score indicators to differentiate metadata from actionable rectangular targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#10233F` Navy fill, `#FFFFFF` text, 8px border radius, 12px vertical and 24px horizontal padding. Hover shifts to `#0B172A`. Active state depresses slightly with an inset tone. Focus state renders a sharp 2px outline in `#2457D6` with a 2px offset.
- **Secondary Action**: Bordered button with 1px `#E2E8F0` edge, `#FFFFFF` surface, and `#182338` text. On hover, background shifts to `#F6F8FC` with border darkening to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Accent Action**: Reserved for active test submission or platform checkout: `#2457D6` fill, `#FFFFFF` text. On hover, shifts to `#1D44AA`.
- **Destructive Action**: `#DC2626` text with `#FEE2E2` background, scaling to solid `#DC2626` with `#FFFFFF` text on confirmation states.

### Exam Cards
- **Structure**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border `#E2E8F0`, 12px border radius, internal padding of 24px (`space-lg`).
- **Anatomy**: 
  - **Top Row**: Subject Tag / National Code (e.g., `ESUEE-2016EC`) aligned left; Verification Badge or Difficulty Pill aligned right.
  - **Body**: Exam Title (`headline-sm`, `#182338`), accompanied by sub-tier meta (Total Questions, Time Allowance, Passing Mark) in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).
  - **Footer**: Progress bar with Teal Accent (`#16A6A1`) track or primary test launch button.

### Form Inputs & High-Accessibility Controls
- **Inputs**: 44px min-height, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, 4px radius, `#FFFFFF` background, `#182338` text. Placeholder uses `#94A3B8`. Focus state applies a 1px border `#2457D6` coupled with an accessible `0 0 0 3px rgba(36, 87, 214, 0.15)` focus ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 20px dimension. Unchecked: 1.5px border `#CBD5E1`, `#FFFFFF` fill. Checked: `#2457D6` fill with an optical white check glyph. Focus ring ensures full keyboard navigability for screen readers and high-speed key entry.

### Segmented Filters & Exam Subject Chips
- **Segmented Bar**: Encapsulated in a `#F1F5F9` tray with 6px radius and 4px internal padding. 
- **Filter Tabs**: Inactive state is transparent with `#64748B` text. Active state renders as a crisp `#FFFFFF` pill with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#10233F` text, and subtle elevation (`0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`).
- **Subject Chips**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#F6F8FC` base, `#10233F` text, 1px `#E2E8F0` perimeter border. Hover shifts border to `#2457D6`.

### Verification Badges & Status Indicators
- **Verified Official Badge**: `#DCFCE7` background, `#15803D` text, 1px `#BBF7D0` outline with an integrated shield/check icon.
- **Ministry / Regional Indicator**: Soft muted navy badge (`#E0E7FF` surface, `#1E3A8A` text) designating official curriculum alignment.
- **Status Pills**: 12px font size, 4px vertical by 10px horizontal padding, font-weight 600, tabular figures for percentiles and ratings.

### Document Preview Chrome
- **Header Toolbar**: Dark slate or `#0B172A` header housing zoom level, full-page toggles, question navigation jumping, and watermark validation markers.
- **Preview Canvas**: Centered sheet with a clean border, subtle page shadow, and clear pagination footers displaying academic citations and copyright indicators.

### Transparent Pricing & Access Cards
- **Layout**: Clean grid placement with explicit distinction between Free Tier and Pro Institutional Access.
- **Card Styling**: Highlighted Pro tier uses a 2px `#2457D6` perimeter border with an overarching "Recommended for University Matriculation" banner in `#2457D6` with `#FFFFFF` text. Feature checklists utilize `#16A6A1` check icons and tabular ETB / USD pricing figures.
